# UAT checklist

Use a phone and a tablet outdoors, in light mode and dark mode. These checks are manual. The automated suite does not cover a real device, a soft keyboard, or a real printer.

- Sign in, pick the active project, and confirm the project name is readable in the header.
- Steel inventory: add an inward with diameter, length, and quantity. Confirm the row has numbers and no price, rate, or currency.
- Edit a stock row inline and cancel. Delete asks Yes and No. No leaves the row.
- Run the optimizer with a diameter that is not in the yard. The banner should name that diameter and open Add Stock for it.
- Run it again with more bars than the yard holds. The banner should say how many are needed and how many are in stock.
- Commit a cut. Confirm the bar count drops, a long offcut becomes a remnant, and a short offcut stays scrap on the ledger.
- Delete the batch as report only, then delete another with restore. Restored stock comes back. A remnant already used by a later batch does not.
- Ledger: open Audit Ledger, Material Sourcing Breakdown, Batch Orders, and Order Requests. Submit a request, approve one, reject one.
- Site Inventory: receive material, issue it, return part of it, transfer to another store, and record a stocktake.
- Users: create a user, deactivate them, and confirm you cannot deactivate yourself.
- Roles: a system role cannot be edited. A duplicate role name is rejected.
- As Accountant and Sales Executive, steel inward is refused and Add Team Member is not shown.
- Print the ledger report and a batch Foreman, Executive, and Summary sheet. No currency while financials are off. The ledger has three sign-off lines.
- At 360px wide, the page does not scroll sideways, drawers cover the width, and buttons are easy to tap with a thumb.
